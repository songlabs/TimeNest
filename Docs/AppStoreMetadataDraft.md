## English (`en`)

- App Name: TimeNest
- Subtitle: Local calendar and holidays
- Promotional Text: Plan your day with a simple local calendar, schedule management, and public holiday subscriptions.
- Keywords: calendar,schedule,holiday,planner,week,month,local
- Description:

TimeNest is a local-first calendar app for everyday schedule management.

Use month, week, and day views to review your plans, add or edit events, and keep public holidays visible through supported ICS holiday subscriptions. TimeNest supports Japanese, Simplified Chinese, English, and Korean, and stores your schedules and settings locally by default.

Current features:
- Month, week, and day calendar views
- Local event creation, editing, and deletion
- Holiday subscriptions for Japan, China, Korea, and the United States
- Language settings for Japanese, Simplified Chinese, English, Korean, and system mode
- Local-first data handling
- Manual TimeNest file import and export

- Release Notes: Initial TestFlight/App Store candidate with local calendar views, event management, holiday subscriptions, multilingual resources, and local-first data handling.
- Review Notes: TimeNest does not require account login. Holiday subscription sync uses public HTTPS ICS URLs selected in the app. The app can launch and manage local events offline.
